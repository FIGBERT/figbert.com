I was wandering through a little Austin bookstore this past summer,
browsing and looking for a card to send to a friend, when a title [I had
seen before] jumped out at me. [_On Tyranny_] was compact and cheap, and
I bought it.

Regardless of how one may feel about the contents of the book—perhaps
that it is useful, perhaps that it is overly self-important, [many
things can be true at once]—I am just glad to be back in an era of
[pamphlets]. Go write down your thoughts in a little booklet and publish
them.
